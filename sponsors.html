Sponsorship

  • 54% more marine heatwave days than a century ago
  • 8of 10 most severe events on record have happened since 2010
  • 34+ events with documented social and economic impacts, in every ocean basin
  • $3.1bn a year in indirect losses, from a single event

Source: Smith, Burrows, Hobday, Sen Gupta, Moore, Thomsen, Wernberg and Smale, Socioeconomic impacts of marine heatwaves, Science 374 (2021), whose authors include members of this symposium's organising team.

IMHWS-1 is the first international meeting devoted entirely to marine heatwaves — putting the oceanographers, ecologists, modellers and managers who work on ocean temperature extremes in one room for the first time. We expect 200 to 250 delegates, most from overseas.

The symposium has no society behind it and no membership income. Sponsorship keeps registration affordable and funds the students and early-career researchers who would otherwise not get to Perth.

Amounts are being set alongside the budget and will be published in the first circular. Named packages are also available for the welcome reception, poster sessions, plenary sessions and student prizes, as are in-kind contributions — catering, audiovisual, local transport or field-trip support. We are happy to build something that does not fit these categories.

Sponsorship carries no influence over the scientific programme. Abstracts are selected on their merits, keynotes are invited by the committee, and delegates' contact details are never passed on.
