Sponsorship

IMHWS-1 is the first international meeting devoted entirely to marine heatwaves — putting the oceanographers, ecologists, modellers and managers who work on ocean temperature extremes in one room for the first time. We expect 200 to 250 delegates, most from overseas.

Those delegates are physical oceanographers, marine ecologists, modellers, fisheries and aquaculture scientists, and the agency staff and managers who deal with these events when they happen. Keynote speakers will include XXX (XXX) and XXX (XXX).

The symposium has no society behind it and no membership income. Sponsorship keeps registration affordable and funds the students and early-career researchers who would otherwise not get to Perth. We expect them to make up XXX of the meeting, and keeping registration within their reach is the most direct thing a sponsor does here.
