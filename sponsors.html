Sponsorship & support

IMHWS-1 is the first international meeting devoted entirely to marine heatwaves. It brings together the physical oceanographers, ecologists, modellers, social scientists and managers working on ocean temperature extremes — communities that publish in different journals and rarely share a room.

Why support this meeting

Marine heatwaves have moved in a decade from a curiosity to one of the defining problems in ocean science, with consequences for fisheries, aquaculture, reef management, insurance and coastal communities. The research community working on them has grown accordingly, and until now has had no meeting of its own.

Sponsorship puts your organisation in front of that community at the point it first assembles, and directly funds the participation of people who would otherwise not be able to travel.

Who will be there

Attendance figures will be confirmed once registration opens. The expressions of interest received so far come from across the following communities.

Research

Physical oceanography, marine ecology, biogeochemistry, climate modelling and prediction, from universities and national agencies worldwide.

Government & management

Fisheries and marine park agencies, environmental departments, and the meteorological and oceanographic services running operational forecasts.

Industry

Aquaculture and fisheries operators, observing-system and instrumentation companies, and the consultancies and insurers who price ocean risk.

What sponsorship does not buy

So that it is clear from the outset: sponsorship carries no influence over the scientific programme. Abstracts are selected on their merits, keynote invitations are made by the organising committee, and no sponsor is given a speaking slot in the scientific sessions as part of a package. We will not pass on delegates' contact details.

We expect prospective sponsors to regard that as a feature. A meeting whose programme can be bought is not worth sponsoring.
