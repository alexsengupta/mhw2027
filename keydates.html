Key dates

The symposium dates themselves are fixed. Everything else on this page is the organising team's working plan, published early because people need to apply for funding, request leave and start visa applications long before a conference programme exists.

These dates are indicative

Every date below except 20–23 September 2027 is a target, not a commitment. Deadlines of this kind routinely move, and the abstract deadline in particular is the one most likely to be extended. Nothing here should be treated as final until it appears in the first circular. Plan against these dates; do not book non-refundable travel against them.

    Abstract submission and early-bird registration open

    Both open together. Submissions cover talks and posters, and there is no separate call for sessions — the programme is assembled from the abstracts received, so the abstract is the only thing you need to prepare. Opening in December is deliberate: it lets anyone on a calendar-year budget register before their 2026 funds expire.

Worth starting early

Australian visas

Almost every non-Australian delegate will need a visa, and processing times vary from a couple of weeks to several months depending on where you apply. Start well before the early-bird deadline rather than after it.

Letters of invitation

We can provide a letter of invitation to support a visa or funding application. Write to IMHWS2027@gmail.com with your name as it appears in your passport, your institution and your intended dates.

Getting to Perth

Perth Airport has direct connections to Asia, the Middle East, Europe and the east coast of Australia, and is about 25 minutes from the venue. Fares are usually better booked well ahead for late September.

Every date above other than the symposium itself is subject to change until it is confirmed here. The most reliable way to hear about each one is to register an expression of interest.
