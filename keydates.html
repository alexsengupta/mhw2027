Worth starting early

Australian visas

Almost every non-Australian delegate will need a visa, and processing times vary from a couple of weeks to several months depending on where you apply. Start well before the early-bird deadline rather than after it.

Letters of invitation

We can provide a letter of invitation to support a visa or funding application. Write to IMHWS2027@gmail.com with your name as it appears in your passport, your institution and your intended dates.

Getting to Perth

Perth Airport has direct connections to Asia, the Middle East, Europe and the east coast of Australia, and is about 25 minutes from the venue. Fares are usually better booked well ahead for late September.

Every date above other than the symposium itself is subject to change until it is confirmed here. The most reliable way to hear about each one is to register an expression of interest.
